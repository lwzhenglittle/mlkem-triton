## What the correction was worth

The old column over-counted the CPU by **1.25x to 4.25x, median 1.94x** -- i.e. every ratio in it made the device look 0.2x to 0.8x worse than it is.

No primitive row changed side of 1.0x. One signature row did, and it is not in
the table above because it is not a `bench_primitive` row: **ML-DSA-44 verify, 0.57x -> 1.60x**
(0.53 Mops/s on the device against 331.3 k ops/s from the barrier; the old 929 k
was the pool's sum of minima). Falcon-512's device core goes 3.72x -> 6.1x
(1.65 Mops/s against 272.3 k ops/s barrier vs 443 k old; 5.76x in
`benchmarks/snapshot-20261127T140302Z.md` at 1.57 Mops/s).
